Core System Modules

Designed to help students identify their technical strengths and prepare for entry-level tech roles.

Automated Resume Extraction

Upload your existing PDF resume. The system extracts text using PyPDF2, scans against 80+ technical skills, and pre-populates your profile with manual fallback options.

Upload Resume

Machine Learning Matcher

Converts your skills and interests into TF-IDF vector space, computing Cosine Similarity against benchmark career profiles. Shows Top 3 matches with skill gaps and reasoning.

View Recommendations

Interactive Learning Roadmaps

Follow curated 3-phase roadmaps (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) with suggested projects, topics to master, and an interactive checklist that saves progress.

View Roadmaps

Visual Analytics Dashboard

Chart.js powered radar graphs, Doughnut charts for acquired vs missing skills, and bar comparisons across all tech disciplines to highlight growth areas.

Open Analytics

Demonstrative Job Portal

System Workflow: From Input to Career Readiness

1

Enter Skills / Resume

Upload your PDF resume or manually pick your technical skills and interests.

2

TF-IDF Vectorization

The ML model extracts term frequency and inverse document frequency weights.

3

Cosine Similarity Match

Calculates cosine distance between profile vector and 10+ career benchmarks.

4

Roadmap & Job Fit

Inspect missing skills, explore step-by-step project tasks, and see matching demo jobs.

Academic Project Notice: Recommendation scores are algorithmic estimates measuring profile and skill similarity using TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity. They represent suitability metrics rather than guaranteed real-world hiring probabilities.
